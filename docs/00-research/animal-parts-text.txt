Small Animal Parts
When you give your Chao a Small Animal, it absorbs not only stats, but takes on features of the animals appearance. Certain animals can change speicific body parts of your Chao. If you give them more animals, the body parts can be replaced by new parts.
On an evolved Chao, animal parts are bigger, more pronounced and usually a different colour too.
This page will list what animals give which body parts. There are seven animal part slots on Sonic Adventure 1 / DX, and eight slots on Sonic Adventure 2 / Battle:
Arms
Ears
Forehead (includes eyebrows)
Face [SA2/B only]
Horns
Feet
Tail
Wings
There is a great section on the
3D Chao Viewer
where you can add animal parts to your Chao to see how they look! Make sure you check it out!
